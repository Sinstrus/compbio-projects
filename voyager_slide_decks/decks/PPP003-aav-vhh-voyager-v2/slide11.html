Three-Stage Validation Pipeline

1

Binding Assays

  • ELISA: VHH3-TNSALP binding
  • SPR: Binding kinetics (Kd)
  • Western: VHH3 display confirm
  • Cryo-EM: Capsid integrity

Go/No-Go: Kd < 10 nM

2

Cell Transduction

  • MC3T3: Osteoblast targeting
  • HepG2: Liver detargeting
  • HEK293: Production QC
  • FACS: GFP+ quantification

Go/No-Go: >3x bone vs liver

3

Functional Output

  • ALP assay: Enzyme activity
  • qPCR: ALPL mRNA levels
  • ICC: Protein localization
  • Mineralization: ARS staining

Go/No-Go: >2x ALP activity

Week 3-4: Transduction

Week 5-6: Functional
