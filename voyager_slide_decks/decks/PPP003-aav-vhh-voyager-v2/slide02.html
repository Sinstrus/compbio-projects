The Problem: AAV9 Broad Tropism

Current Limitations

  • Systemic biodistribution - AAV9 transduces liver, heart, CNS
  • High dose requirements - 10^14 vg/kg needed for effect
  • Off-target toxicity - Liver transduction causes hepatotoxicity
  • Immune responses - Broad exposure increases NAbs

Clinical Need

  • Hypophosphatasia (HPP) - Rare bone disorder (1:100,000)
  • ALPL deficiency - ERT requires frequent infusions
  • Bone-specific delivery - Target osteoblasts, avoid liver
  • Long-term expression - Single-dose gene therapy goal

Goal: Engineer AAV capsids that specifically target bone while reducing off-target transduction
