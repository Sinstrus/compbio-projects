Three-Stage Validation Pipeline

1

Western Blot

Physical Confirmation

  • Does modified VP express?
  • Correct MW shift? (VP + ~15 kDa for VHH)
  • Stoichiometry intact?

Go/No-Go: VHH-VP band at expected size

2

Binding Assay

Functional VHH

  • Does VHH bind ALPL?
  • Above WT background?
  • Dose-dependent?

Method: Flow cytometry on ALPL+ cells

Go/No-Go: Signal above WT control

3

Transduction

Tropism Shift

  • Enhanced transduction of ALPL+ cells?
  • Compare to WT AAV9 baseline

Success: Significant increase in ALPL+ cell transduction

If Stage 3 passes: Proceed to in vivo biodistribution in mouse model
