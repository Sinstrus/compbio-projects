Why B3? The Library Problem

Challenge: Current N-terminal insertion approach (Ideas A/B) hardcodes a single VHH sequence

Ideas A/B (Current)

  • VHH inserted at N-terminus of VP1 or VP2
  • Requires co-transfection with WT cap plasmid
  • Fixed VHH:VP ratio determined by plasmid ratio
  • Problem: One VHH = one plasmid

Library Challenge

  • VHH discovery requires testing many variants
  • Each variant needs separate cloning
  • Co-transfection limits scalability
  • Bottleneck: Cannot easily screen libraries

B3 Solution

  • VHH in retained intron = single plasmid
  • Splicing controls VHH:VP ratio automatically
  • Easy VHH swapping via restriction sites
  • Advantage: Library-compatible design
