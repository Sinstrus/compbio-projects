Why Intron Retention?

  • Controls VHH:VP ratio through splicing efficiency
  • Single-plasmid system (no co-transfection)
  • Tuneable display rate via splice site strength
  • Maintains wild-type VP production

Design Requirements

  • Weak splice sites (10-25% retention target)
  • X region must preserve reading frame
  • No stop codons in retained intron
  • Acceptable amino acids from polyY tract
