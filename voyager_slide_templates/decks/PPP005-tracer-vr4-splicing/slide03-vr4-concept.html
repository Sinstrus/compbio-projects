WHY VR4 LOOP?

  • Surface-exposed variable region
  • Common to VP1, VP2, VP3
  • Tolerates small insertions (5-15 aa)
  • Higher VHH copy number than VP2

DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

  • Spliced scar must be small (<10 aa)
  • Reading frame preserved in both states
  • No stop codons when retained
  • 10-20% retention target
