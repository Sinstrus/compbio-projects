Extending to N-Terminal Positions

FROM VR4 TO N-TERMINAL

Having validated intron retention at VR4 with 41 constructs, we now extend the same 492 bp cassette to N-terminal VP1/VP2 unique regions — offering flexible, disordered sites for larger VHH payloads.

VR4 Loop Insertion (Established)

  • Capsid surface-exposed loop
  • Structurally constrained — small peptides only
  • Present on all 60 VP subunits
  • Risk of disrupting capsid assembly
  • Our AVD059-099 series: intron retention in VR4

Best for: short peptides, epitope display

N-Terminal Insertion (New)

  • Flexible, disordered unique region
  • Tolerates large domains (164 aa VHH + linkers)
  • VP1: 5 copies / VP2: 5 copies per capsid
  • Externalized through VP1/VP2 N-terminal pore
  • Proven: AAV-DJ VP2 fusions in literature

Best for: VHH domains, large functional payloads
