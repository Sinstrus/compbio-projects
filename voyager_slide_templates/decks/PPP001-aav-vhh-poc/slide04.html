Three VHH Display Architectures Under Test

Architecture 1: VR4 Loop

Insert site: VR-IV surface loop (shared region)

VHH on: VP1, VP2, and VP3

Copy number: ~60/capsid (highest)

Pro: Maximum display valency

Con: Higher assembly risk

Architecture 2: VP1 N-term

Insert site: VP1-unique N-terminus (after M-A)

VHH on: VP1 only

Copy number: ~5/capsid

Pro: Minimal VP2/VP3 impact, flexible site

Note: Lower valency than VR4

Architecture 3: VP2 N-term

Insert site: VP2-unique N-terminus (after M-A)

VHH on: VP2 only

Copy number: ~5/capsid

Pro: Alternative VP-selective approach

Tests: VP2 insertion tolerance
