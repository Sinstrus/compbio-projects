Phase I POC: Validate the Display Platform

Three key questions this POC will answer:

1

Functional Virus?

Can we produce functional virus with VHH displayed?

Modified capsids must still assemble, package genome, and transduce cells

2

Binding Function?

Does the displayed VHH retain binding function?

VHH must fold correctly when fused to VP and bind target (ALPL) with meaningful affinity

3

Altered Tropism?

Does VHH display alter viral tropism?

Expect enhanced transduction of ALPL+ cells vs wild-type AAV9

POC Binder: Anti-ALPL VHH3 — validated binder with cross-species reactivity (human/mouse)
