Mosaic vs Trans-Complementation

Philosophy 1: Mosaic (Spike-in)

How it works: Mix VHH-modified + WT at controlled DNA ratio

Result: Partial VHH incorporation; mix of modified + unmodified VPs

Pros:

Safer, hedges assembly risk

Cons:

Heterogeneous population

Philosophy 2: Trans-Complementation

How it works: KO helper supplies unmodified VPs; VHH construct supplies modified VP

Result: 100% of target VP type has VHH; homogeneous composition

Pros:

Clean composition, quantifiable

Cons:

Requires additional helper constructs
