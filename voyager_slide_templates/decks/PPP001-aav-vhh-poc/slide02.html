The Opportunity: AAV Capsids as a Binder Display Platform

The Concept

  • Display binders on AAV capsid surface — analogous to phage display
  • Screen for functional binding in vivo — altered tropism = binder works
  • Identify winning binders, then optimize independently — AAV is the discovery tool, not the product
  • Future vision: Library-based receptor discovery and in vivo binder screening

Why VHH Nanobodies?

  • Single-domain format: No light chain — simpler engineering, compact insert (~15 kDa)
  • High stability: Tolerates insertion into structured capsid regions
  • Modular and well-characterized: Easy to swap different VHH sequences into same architecture

Key insight: VHH nanobodies are ideal for capsid display due to their small size, stability, and modularity.
