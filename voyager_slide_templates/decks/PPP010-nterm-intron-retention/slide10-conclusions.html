Key Conclusions

N-terminal VP1/VP2 positions accommodate full VHH domains (164 aa) with GGGGS linkers, far exceeding VR4 loop capacity.

3

AVD133 (VP1) inserts at the PGAPQP natural linker, preserving all three VPs native with exonic placement for VP1.

4

Both constructs pass all verification checks: frame, scar identity, junction safety, restriction sites, and synthesis screen.
