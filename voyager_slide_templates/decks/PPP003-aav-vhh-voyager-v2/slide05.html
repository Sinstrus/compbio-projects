Two Insertion Strategies

Strategy A: VP1 N-terminus

Site: After M1 | Display: ~5 copies/virion

Advantages:

  • VP1-unique region accessible on surface
  • Lower copy number preserves tropism
  • PLA2 domain unaffected

Considerations:

  • Limited display valency
  • May require helper optimization

Strategy B: VP2 N-terminus

Site: After T138 | Display: ~5 copies/virion

Advantages:

  • VP2-unique region also accessible
  • Independent of VP1 modifications
  • Can combine with Strategy A

Considerations:

  • VP2 start codon is ACG (non-canonical)
  • May affect VP2:VP3 ratio

Design: Both strategies tested independently and combined to optimize bone targeting.
