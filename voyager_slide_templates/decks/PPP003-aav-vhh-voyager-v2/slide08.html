Two Production Strategies

Cis Complementation

Method: Single helper encodes all modified VPs

Components:

  • pRep2Cap9-VP1-VHH3 (modified VP1)
  • Native VP2 and VP3 from single ORF

Advantages:

  • Simpler workflow (2 plasmids)
  • Consistent VP ratios

Limitations:

  • 100% VP1 modified (may affect titer)

Trans Complementation (Mosaic)

Method: Separate plasmids for modified/WT capsid

Components:

  • pRep2Cap9-VP1-VHH3 (targeting)
  • pRep2Cap9-WT co-transfected

Advantages:

  • Tunable targeting (10-50% modified)
  • Preserves native tropism

Limitations:

  • More complex (3 plasmids)

Recommendation: Start with cis for proof-of-concept, optimize with trans mosaic for production.
