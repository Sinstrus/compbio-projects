Two Virus Production Strategies

Mosaic Capsids

METHOD

Co-transfect wt cap + VHH-cap plasmids at 1:1 ratio

RESULT

Mixed capsids with partial VHH display (~50% of VP subunits)

PRO

Simpler production

CON

Variable stoichiometry

Trans-Complementation

METHOD

Co-transfect VP-KO helper + VP-VHH plasmids at 1:1 ratio

RESULT

100% of specified VP has VHH; others remain native

PRO

Defined stoichiometry

CON

More complex design

CONFIDENTIAL
